Research

Democracy, institutions, and political change.

My research interests center on comparative politics, democratic institutions, political parties, electoral systems, democratic erosion, and European politics.

University of Notre Dame

Approach

I am especially interested in how political institutions shape democratic competition — and how parties, elections, and institutional design can contribute to democratic resilience or erosion.
